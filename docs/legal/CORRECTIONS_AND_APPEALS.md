# Corrections and appeals policy

**Applies to:** every claim, dossier, export, or public statement produced by Project X-Ray.
**Owner:** Corrections owner — the repository maintainer (`Kartik24Hulmukh`) until a named editor is appointed.
**Contact:** open a GitHub issue titled `correction:` or use the security contact in `SECURITY.md` for private matters.

## Service levels (SLA)

| Stage | Commitment |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | Within **2 business days** of receipt |
| Interim action for a credible allegation of factual error about a person or authority | Claim moved out of any publishable state (`published` → `under_review`) within **24 hours** of acknowledgement |
| Decision with written reasons | Within **10 business days** |
| Appeal | A second, independent reviewer who did not take the original decision; decision within **10 further business days** |
| Correction record | Permanent, dated correction notice attached to the dossier and recorded in the tamper-evident audit log |

## Principles

1. **Corrections are evidence events.** A correction cites the source that establishes the error, exactly as the original claim cited its source.
2. **Silence is not denial.** A record that cannot be located is reported as not located; it is never restated as absent.
3. **No self-review.** The person who authored or approved a claim cannot decide its correction or appeal.
4. **Synthetic preview.** During the 16–17 September 2026 preview all dossiers are synthetic; this policy nevertheless applies in full so that the process is rehearsed before any real case.

## What we publish about corrections

A quarterly corrections ledger: number received, acknowledged inside SLA, upheld, rejected, appealed, and median time to decision.
